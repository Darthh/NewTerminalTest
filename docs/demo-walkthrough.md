# Demo walkthrough

1. Open `/about`, then **Start a conversation**. The navy terminal shell and centered chat composer use the default Luna appearance. Change themes in Appearance and reload to confirm your preference.
2. Ask **Compare NVDA and SPY over the last year**. Read the explicit illustrative-data date, compare the stated returns, and inspect both inline quote cards. Drag a chart interval in either direction; its measurement remains after release.
3. Use header search to open **AMD** or **NVDA**. Switch ranges, compare SPY, enable volume or the moving average, and add the symbol to your watchlist.
4. Open the market dashboard. Remove a widget, add one, change its width and order, then reload. Removing every widget intentionally leaves an empty dashboard that also survives reload.
5. Open Finance CRM. Add a fictional client, select a stage and risk profile, record a note, and schedule a review. Reopen the record after a reload. Records are saved only in this browser.
6. In Client Portfolios, choose **Add New**. Enter a name and holdings, associate your client, and review allocations. More than 100% is rejected; an allocation below 100% leaves labeled cash. Share-based values remain unavailable without quote inputs.
7. In Reports, choose **Create Report**. Select Standard, One Pager, or Comparison; pick a template and saved portfolio; add your details. Edit a page, reorder it with the arrows, hide a page, and save a template or report. Export PDF and CSV, then reopen the report. Exports preserve the visible order and continue long content.
8. Attach a TXT or Markdown research note in chat. Request relevant excerpts and inspect the filename/chunk citations. PDF, image, and audio extraction remain unavailable.
9. Open Research history to search, rename, reopen, or delete a conversation. Start New Chat to clear the active transcript while preserving the archive. Private cloud storage, live providers, web search, hosted models, and scheduled notifications need further implementation/configuration.

All starter clients, allocations, quotes, historical price series, filings, and event calendars are fictional or illustrative. This preview does not place trades or provide personalized investment instructions.
